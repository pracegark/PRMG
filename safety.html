PRIME RESOURCES MANAGEMENT GROUP

Committed to safety

We work to protect employees, reduce incidents, and deliver quality work in compliance with applicable occupational health and safety requirements.

Experienced safety leadership

Our full-time safety representative helps us provide customers with safety-conscious employees. The representative works with clients from the start of the relationship, including visiting facilities and coordinating with client safety staff to understand job-specific needs and prepare employees for their work environment.

Our safety representative brings 10 years of safety experience and 7 years of safety management experience, along with experience as a Radiation Safety Officer, safety instructor, and Medic First Aid instructor. The representative also has 25 years of volunteer fire department service and 20 years as a paramedic.

Training and pre-hire screening

Training and screening are arranged to meet client requirements. Pre-hire screening may include:

  • Hazmat and PHMSA
  • Drug and alcohol testing
  • DOT and non-DOT screening
  • Routine fit-for-duty evaluations
  • Pulmonary function testing
  • Respirator fit testing

Safety training

Depending on the work and client requirements, training can be provided in-house or through a certified third-party training facility. Available training includes 40-hour Hazmat, annual Hazmat refreshers, confined space, Medic First Aid, API RP 2D rigger training, RSO and NORM training, lead awareness, fall protection, and OSHA 10-hour training.
