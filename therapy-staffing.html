Prime Therapy

Prime has been a leader in labor solutions for businesses for over a decade. Prime is locally owned and based in Broussard, Louisiana. Prime is now bringing this leadership and experience to provide therapy services to various healthcare providers. Noticing a pattern of average outcomes in inpatient/outpatient settings and in our communities was the fundamental force in the creation of Prime Therapy Staffing Solutions.

Prime Therapy Staffing Solution specializes in staffing high quality physical, occupational, and speech therapist in all areas of healthcare. PTSS is committed to quality services to patients while delivering feasible solutions to our customers. Our principals have over a decade of providing high quality therapy services with a large spectrum of specific higher education therapy techniques . PTSS is committed to providing a quality service increasing the wellbeing of all patients serviced while providing individualized healthcare to enable maximizing outcomes while increasing the quality of life that all patients deserve. Our staff provide services which are efficient and geared towards allowing the recipient of our care to function at their maximum potential in the quickest manner while also bringing forth a standard of compassion and customer satisfaction that is Prime to the industry.

PTSS is dedicated to:

  • Provision of high-quality and compassionate medical services to the patients
  • Utilization of high-quality staff who will achieve the services in a professional manner
  • Bringing high quality, feasible solutions to our customers
  • Working with customers to customize administrative and operational procedures to blend into and work with their existing services
  • Achieve maximum results in an efficient, timely manner while holding to the high-quality, compassionate standards of Prime

The combination of principals who are certified and licensed in the industry, along with the expertise and leadership of administration and business management, assures our customers that both the healthcare and the business side of the relationship will be handled in the most professional, high-quality manner possible. We are bringing solutions to our customers in the healthcare industry while delivering high-quality medical services to the patients.
